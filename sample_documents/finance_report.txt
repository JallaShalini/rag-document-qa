Q2 2026 Finance Report

Executive Summary:

Revenue grew by 14% compared to Q1, driven by increased subscription sales and new enterprise contracts. Operating expenses remained stable, while gross margin improved from 47% to 52%.

Key Metrics:
- Total revenue: $4.8M
- Operating expenses: $2.1M
- Net profit: $900K
- Customer retention: 89%

Highlights:
- Subscription renewals increased by 18%.
- New product launch contributed $450K in new annual recurring revenue.
- Marketing expenses were contained at 11% of revenue.

Action Items:
- Continue investment in sales enablement to support enterprise expansion.
- Tighten inventory management to preserve margin.
- Evaluate the pilot ERP automation tool for Q3 deployment.
