# Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to Acme Solutions! We are committed to providing a positive, inclusive, and productive workplace.

## Company Values

- Integrity: We act honestly and ethically in all situations.
- Collaboration: We work together to achieve shared goals.
- Innovation: We encourage new ideas and continuous learning.

## Work Hours

Standard office hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Flexible scheduling is available with manager approval.

## Remote Work

Employees may request remote work up to two days per week. Remote work arrangements should be documented and approved by HR.

## Code of Conduct

All employees must treat colleagues with respect, comply with company policies, and maintain the confidentiality of internal information.

## Benefits

The company provides health insurance, retirement savings plans, paid time off, and professional development resources.
